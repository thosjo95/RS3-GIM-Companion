RS3 GIM Companion: the Group Ironman tracker for RuneScape 3

A free dashboard for your RuneScape 3 Group Ironman team. Add your group once and follow every member together: XP gains per day, week, month and year, skills and levels, boss kills, rare drops, clue scrolls, shared goals and a leaderboard inside your group.

The Global Leaderboard ranks every tracked Regular, Hardcore and Competitive Group Ironman group: top groups and players of the week, month and year, clue scrolls, 99s and 120s.

Works from the public RuneScape hiscores and the Adventurer's Log. No download, no account.
